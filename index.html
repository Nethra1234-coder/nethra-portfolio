03 — Research

Adaptive Speculative Reasoning

Research internship on step-level confidence scoring and calibration for reasoning chains generated by a small language model, evaluated across GSM8K and HotpotQA. My work: merging confidence scores with correctness labels, computing Expected Calibration Error and reliability diagrams, and producing a recommended confidence threshold (θ) for deciding when a reasoning step should be trusted outright versus escalated for a second pass.

Earlier on the project I did manual QA and data curation — reviewing AI-generated reasoning chains, categorising failure types, and curating gold examples that later fed the calibration analysis.
